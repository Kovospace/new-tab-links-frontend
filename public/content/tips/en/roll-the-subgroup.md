# Roll up a subgroup

The links in a subgroup can be hidden by rolling the subgroup up: click its header, or choose "Expand / Collapse group" in its menu (three dot icon).

![The "AI" subgroup in a group on the new tab](../../../images/en/tips/roll-the-subgroup/podskupina-lokacia_1x.webp)

Then only the number of hidden items is shown, which saves vertical space.

![An unrolled subgroup with all its links](../../../images/en/tips/roll-the-subgroup/podskupina-rozbalena_1x.webp)

![A rolled-up subgroup with the number of hidden items](../../../images/en/tips/roll-the-subgroup/podskupina-zrolovana_1x.webp)

The setting is remembered, so a rolled or unrolled subgroup stays that way, on all your synchronized devices.
