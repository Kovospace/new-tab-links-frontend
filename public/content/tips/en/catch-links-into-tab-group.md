# Catch links into a tab group

A subgroup can pull tabs into a Chrome tab group of its own. Once it does, every tab that lands on
one of the subgroup's links joins that group — whether you opened it from the new tab page or
followed a link from somewhere else.

## Turning it on

1. Open the subgroup's menu.
2. Turn on **Catch links into tab group**.

From then on the subgroup keeps its tabs together, in the subgroup's own colour.
