# Let's begin

Right after installation, a new tab opens straight into your first space, "My space". It is still empty, so it offers a few ways to fill it:

![Your first space, with the ways to fill it](../../../images/en/tips/lets-begin/1_1x.webp)

- **Create new group** - start with a group of your own and add links to it.
- **Import bookmarks from the browser** - each folder you tick becomes a group, and its subfolders become subgroups. The browser asks you to allow access to your bookmarks only at this moment.
- **Log in / set up sync** - if you already use Tabilinks elsewhere, bring your links from your account. Choose "I have a username and password" or "I have a sync code".

From then on, your links are what you will see every time you open a new tab.

![New tab screen with groups of links](../../../images/en/tips/lets-begin/2_1x.webp)

The panel on the left holds your open tabs, closed tabs and history, and it is open beside your links from the start.

If you would rather have the room, uncheck "Keep open" in the panel's head: it folds into a strip of icons along the left edge. Rest the mouse on the strip for a moment (0.3 seconds by default; you can change this in the settings) and the panel unfolds again.

![New tab screen with the history of closed tabs unfolded](../../../images/en/tips/lets-begin/3_1x.webp)

Now explore the menus wherever you see the "three vertical dots" symbol.

![New tab screen, menu example](../../../images/en/tips/lets-begin/4_1x.webp)

Links have a context menu of their own too, opened with a right click.

![Link context menu, opened with a right click](../../../images/en/tips/lets-begin/5_1x.webp)
