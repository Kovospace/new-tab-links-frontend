# Choose a light or dark theme

The extension can be drawn light or dark. Choose which in the extension settings: under the "UI and behavior" section, set "Theme" to one of:

- **Follow the system** – switches along with your operating system's light or dark mode. This is the default.
- **Always light**
- **Always dark**

The change applies straight away, in every new tab you have open.

## Flat or glass

Right below it, "Theme style" decides how the groups, panels and menus are drawn:

- **Aero** – partly transparent, blurring the background picture behind them, like frosted glass. This is the default.
- **Flat** – solid surfaces.

Both work with either theme, so there are four combinations to choose from.

These settings are not synchronized; they are remembered only in the browser where you set them.
