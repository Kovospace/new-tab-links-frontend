# Cookies

*Effective {effectiveDate}*

<br>

**This website does not use cookies**, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking
scripts from anyone else.

It does store a few small entries in your browser's own storage (`localStorage` and
`sessionStorage`). They are read only by this website and sent nowhere else.

| Name | Where | What it holds | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `newtablinks.session` | localStorage | Your sign-in tokens | Keeps you signed in; only present while you are | Until you sign out |
| `newtablinks.language` | localStorage | The language you chose | Shows the site in your language | Until you clear it |
| `newtablinks.currency` | localStorage | The currency you chose, only once you choose one | Shows prices in your currency | Until you clear it |
| `newtablinks.websiteVisitReportedOn` | localStorage | Today's date, nothing else | Counts your browser at most once a day in the total number of visitors | Replaced daily |
| `newtablinks.adminSession` | sessionStorage | The operator's sign-in | Used only on the operator's own pages | Until the tab is closed |

The session, language, currency and operator entries are strictly necessary for what you ask
the site to do. The visit date lets us know how many people visit, without knowing who: only a daily total is kept, and nothing
that identifies you or your browser. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Other sites

When you choose to sign in with Google or to pay through Creem, you leave this website for theirs,
and they may use cookies under their own policies:
[Google](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies) and
[Creem](https://www.creem.io/privacy).

## Removing the entries

You can remove them at any time by signing out or by clearing this site's data in your browser's
settings. The site keeps working; you will just be signed out and see it in the default
language and currency.
