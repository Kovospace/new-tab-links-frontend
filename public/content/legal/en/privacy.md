## Using the extension without an account

The extension works without an account. Your links, groups and settings then stay in your
browser's own storage and are never sent to us. The extension reports only an anonymous count of
opened new tabs, which is added to a daily total and cannot be linked to you.

## Visitors of the website

- **Registration limit.** A random, anonymous visitor token limits how often one browser can
  register or check whether a username is taken, to stop abuse. We store only its hash, and delete
  it once it expires. Legal basis: legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f).
- **Visitor statistics.** Each browser is counted at most once a day into a total number of
  visitors. We store only the daily total — no address, identifier or browsing history. Legal
  basis: legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f).
- **Technical data.** Delivering pages and answering requests necessarily involves your IP address
  and browser details. The address is held briefly in memory to limit how many requests one
  source can send; we do not store it in our database.

## Who else receives data

We use these providers, only for the purpose given:

- **Brevo** (Sendinblue SAS, France) delivers our emails — activation and password reset. It
  receives your email address and the content of the message.
- **Google** processes your sign-in if you choose "Sign in with Google", under
  [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Creem** is the reseller and Merchant of Record for premium purchases. It collects your payment
  details, name, email and billing address itself, as a controller of that data, under
  [Creem's privacy policy](https://www.creem.io/privacy). It tells us only what we need to activate
  your premium features.

Our servers and database run in the European Union. We do not transfer your data outside the
European Economic Area ourselves; where Google or Creem do, they rely on their own safeguards,
described in their policies.

## How long we keep data

- Your account and everything tied to it — devices, tokens, synchronised content, premium status —
  is kept until you delete the account.
- **Deleting the account on the Account page erases all of it immediately and permanently.** You
  can also ask us to do it by email.
- Visitor tokens are deleted when they expire; activation and reset codes stop working when they
  expire or are used.
- Daily statistics contain no personal data and are kept indefinitely.
- Invoices and payment records are kept by Creem, as the seller, for as long as tax law requires.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to
restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable form. Most of your data is
visible and editable on the Account page, and you can delete the account there yourself. For
anything else write to [{supportEmail}](mailto:{supportEmail}); we answer within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in the Czech Republic
the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů,
[uoou.gov.cz](https://uoou.gov.cz)), or the authority of the EU country you
live in.
