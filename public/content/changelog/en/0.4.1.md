# 0.4.1

*Released: 2026-10-28*

## Most important changes

- Light, dark or as your system: the theme is one click away, on the "Mode" button in the top bar
- Menus no longer run off the screen - near the bottom of the window they open upwards
- The top bar and the search bar fit the window, also on a smaller screen

### The theme, one click away

The top bar has a new **Mode** button, just before your profile. It says which theme is in force -
**Light**, **Dark** or **System** - and switches it straight from the menu, without opening the
settings. It is the same choice as in the settings, kept on this device only.

- [Choose a light or dark theme](../../tips/en/choose-theme.md)

### Also in this release

- A group's or subgroup's menu at the foot of a long column opens above its button when there is no
  room below, and scrolls when there is room on neither side.
- The workspace heading and its search bar stay inside the window instead of running under the side
  panel, and the top bar no longer wraps on a narrower window.
- The example set has a second subgroup, "Directories", to show how subgroups share a group.
