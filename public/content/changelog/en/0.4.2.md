# 0.4.2

*Released: 2026-10-29*

## Most important changes

- Selecting text in a dialog no longer closes it when you let go of the mouse just outside the field
- The bookmarking popup stays open when you drag over the text in its fields
- Many or wide groups wrap onto the next row instead of running off the screen

### Also in this release

- A dialog now closes on a click beside it only when the whole click happens there. Releasing the
  mouse outside after selecting a link's name or address keeps it open, with everything you typed.
- In the popup that saves the current page, the name field opens with its text selected. Pressing on
  it and moving the mouse a little used to close the popup; now it doesn't.
- Each group is as wide as its links need, up to a limit where long titles are shortened. Groups
  that don't fit on the row move to the next one, so the workspace never scrolls sideways.
- The search bar no longer sits flush against the right edge of the workspace heading.
