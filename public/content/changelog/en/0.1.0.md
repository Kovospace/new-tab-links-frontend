# 0.1.0

*Released: 2026-10-08*

## Most important changes

- The first public release in the Chrome Web Store
- Your own links on every new tab, in profiles, workspaces, groups and subgroups
- Recently closed tabs in a panel on the right edge
- Backup and synchronization across browsers and devices with an account
- Save the page you are on from the toolbar icon, a keyboard shortcut or the right-click menu

Tabilinks replaces the empty new tab page with your own links, organized in a hierarchy: switchable
**profiles**, their **workspaces**, **groups** in each workspace and optional collapsible
**subgroups** inside a group.

![The new tab page with example links](../../../images/en/tips/lets-begin/2_1x.webp)

### Working with links

- **Drag and drop** for moving and sorting groups, subgroups and links.
- **Open all links of a subgroup at once**, and let a subgroup **catch its links into a tab group**
  of its own color, wherever in the browser they are opened from.
- **Save the page you are viewing** from the toolbar icon, a keyboard shortcut you can bind (even
  Ctrl+D) or the right-click menu, which lists the open workspace's groups and subgroups.
- A **search bar** on the new tab page, using the browser's own search engine by default.

### Closed tabs

The panel on the right edge unfolds when the mouse rests there and lists the most recently closed
tabs, ready to reopen or keep as a link.

### Account and synchronization

The extension works fully without an account. Signing in backs your links up and synchronizes them
to every browser and device signed in to the same account.

### Also in this release

- Settings, including the size of the interface.
- Tips about the less obvious features, in a bar on the new tab page.
- Website icons shown beside links.
- Your own background image for premium users.
- English and Slovak.
