# 0.5.0

*Released: 2026-11-15*

## Most important changes

- Favorites: mark the links you use most with a circle, and find them in the side panel and the toolbar popup
- Flash save and a new right-click menu: keep a page with one click or Alt+Shift+F, no questions asked
- Accent colour: seven colours to choose from instead of blue alone
- Resize a group by its corner and sort its links by date or by name

### Favorites

Turn them on in Settings → "Functions" → "Favorites"; they are off until you do. Then every link has
a circle at its end - click it and it turns golden. A link can also be marked from its right-click
menu, or with the "Favorite" checkbox when you add it.

- Closed tabs, history, open tabs and the search suggestions have the circle too. A page that is not
  a link yet opens the save window with "Favorite" already ticked.
- A new **Favorites** card in the side panel lists the favorites of the space you have open. Drag a
  link onto it to mark it.
- The toolbar popup lists them under its form.
- Favorites are synchronised with your other devices, like the links themselves.

- [Discover history](../../tips/en/discover-history.md)

### Flash save and the new right-click menu

Right-click a page or a link, and the **Tabilinks** entry offers **Flash save**, **Add bookmark…** and
**Save to group**.

- **Flash save** keeps the page at once, with no questions, in a group called "Flash save" in the
  space you have open - made the first time you use it. Its keyboard shortcut is **Alt+Shift+F**.
- If the space has no room for the "Flash save" group, the popup tells you so instead of saving.
- In Settings → "UI and behavior" → "Browser context menu actions", choose **Fast-paced link
  grabbing** and Flash save sits alone and straight in the right-click menu, one click away.
  **Standard** keeps the Tabilinks entry with all three.

- [Save the page you are viewing](../../tips/en/saving-from-webpage.md)
- [Save pages with Ctrl+D](../../tips/en/ctrl-d-saves-here.md)

### Accent colour

The colour of active and highlighted buttons, the chosen tab and a focused field is no longer always
blue. Choose blue, indigo, violet, magenta, teal, orange or amber from the swatches in Settings →
"UI and behavior" → "Accent colour". It is remembered per device, like the theme.

### Resize and sort groups

- Drag a group's bottom-right corner to make it wider, narrower, taller or shorter. The size is
  remembered on this device; double-click the corner, or choose "Reset size" in the group's menu, to
  go back to the automatic size.
- "Sort links" in the group's menu puts its links in order once: newest first, oldest first, or by
  name from A to Z or from Z to A. Subgroups keep their places, and their links are sorted too.

### A new start

- There is no first-run screen any more. A new installation opens straight into a starter space,
  "My space", which offers to create a group, to import your bookmarks from the browser - folders
  become groups and subfolders subgroups - or to log in and set up sync. The extension asks for
  access to your bookmarks only when you start the import.
- If you delete every space, a page offers to create a new one, to import a backup or to log in.
- "Log in / set up sync" first asks whether you have a username and password or a sync code.
- A new installation starts on built-in background 02.
- The toolbar button welcomes a new user before anything is set up.

- [Let's begin](../../tips/en/lets-begin.md)

### The side panel

The side panel moved to the left edge, and it is kept open by default. Uncheck "Keep open" in its
head to fold it into a strip of icons again.

- [Discover history](../../tips/en/discover-history.md)

### Settings

A new **Functions** category holds Favorites, Drag and drop and Hide tips. Every category now starts
folded, so the whole list fits on the screen.

- [Hide tips](../../tips/en/hide-tips.md)
- [Use drag-and-drop to organize items](../../tips/en/use-drag-and-drop.md)

### Also in this release

- English says "Space" instead of "Workspace" everywhere.
- An empty group shows a hint, with buttons to add a link or delete the group.
- The theme switcher in the header reads "Theme: …".
- The search bar stays between 16em and 48em wide and fits beside an open side panel.
- The premium slot is only as wide as its banner, and shows a personal note and a "Get PRO" button in
  the page's own colours.
- Close buttons are red.
