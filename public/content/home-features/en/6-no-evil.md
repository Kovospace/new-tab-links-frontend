# Decide what is tracked and what is synced

- The extension works in **"offline first"** mode - it is **ready to use as is**.

- You can be logged in and still have **sync disabled** on a per-device basis.

- We know that history and recent tabs may be sensitive, so:

  - tracking them can be disabled

  - or tracking them can stay enabled while syncing is disabled

  - [Tracking / Sync settings](../../tips/en/setup-privacy.md)

- Even with everything enabled, your data stays in the Tabilinks database and goes nowhere. We are not a big corporation turning Orwell's 1984 into reality.
