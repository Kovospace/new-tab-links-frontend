**Tabilinks Premium** unlocks the full version of the Tabilinks new tab page, on every device you use.

Renews once a year. Cancel whenever you like, and you keep premium until the year you paid for runs out.

### What you get

- **Unlimited links** in your workspaces
- **Unlimited workspaces**, instead of 2 on the free plan
- **Unlimited profiles**, instead of 1
- **Unlimited synchronised installations**, instead of 5. Your links follow you to every computer and browser.
- **500 closed tabs** in the history, instead of 25
- **No ad banner**

"Unlimited" is subject to our [Fair Use Policy](https://tabilinks.app/fair-use). The limits are generous enough that almost nobody will ever reach them.

### Good to know

- If your subscription ends, nothing you created is deleted. Anything over the free limits stays where it is, and you can keep using, editing and deleting it.
- You can change your mind for **14 days** after buying and get a full refund, no reason needed. See the [refund policy](https://tabilinks.app/refunds).
