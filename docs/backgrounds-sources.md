# Built-in backgrounds - where they came from

The sixteen pictures in `public/backgrounds/` are offered behind the new tab page by the
Tabilinks extension (Settings → Background; Kovospace/new-tab-links-extension#127). The
extension ships only 384x216 thumbnails under the same names and downloads the 3840x2160
picture from here when it is chosen - so **a file here is never renamed or renumbered**, and a
replaced one keeps its number.

All of them are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free to use, commercially too, without
attribution. Each was checked to be a free download (not Unsplash+) and to show no people.
Drawings are vector illustrations, rendered at 3840x2160 by Unsplash's image server.
They are offered unnamed; the themes below are for this record only.
